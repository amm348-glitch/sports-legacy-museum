NFL
Football

The most-watched sport in America. The National Football League grew from barnstorming regional teams into an $18 billion annual enterprise that dominates American media, culture, and Sunday afternoons.

By the numbers: 32 Teams — 100M+ Super Bowl Viewers — $18B Annual Revenue — 57 Super Bowls
The Greats

NFL Legends

The quarterbacks, receivers, and defenders who defined football across the decades.

Quarterback · Patriots / Buccaneers
Tom Brady

7× Super Bowl Champion — more than any franchise in NFL history. Brady's 23-year career produced records for passing yards, touchdowns, and sustained excellence that may never be matched.

Quarterback · San Francisco 49ers
Joe Montana

4× Super Bowl Champion, never threw an interception in a Super Bowl. Montana's composure under pressure earned him the nickname "Joe Cool." Many consider him the greatest quarterback of all time.

Quarterback · Kansas City Chiefs
Patrick Mahomes

3× Super Bowl Champion, 2× Super Bowl MVP, 2× League MVP. The most dynamic quarterback of his generation — Mahomes' ability to throw from any angle, at any speed, has redefined what's possible at the position.

Center · Philadelphia Eagles
Jason Kelce

1× Super Bowl Champion, 7× Pro Bowl, 6× First-Team All-Pro. Widely regarded as the greatest center in NFL history. Kelce's combination of athleticism, intelligence, and passion made him the face of the Eagles for 13 seasons.

Quarterback · Green Bay Packers / Jets
Aaron Rodgers

1× Super Bowl Champion, 4× League MVP. Rodgers' touchdown-to-interception ratio is the best in NFL history. His 2011 season — 45 touchdowns, 6 interceptions — is considered the greatest individual QB season ever.

Wide Receiver · San Francisco 49ers
Jerry Rice

The greatest wide receiver of all time — 3× Super Bowl Champion. Rice's 197 receiving touchdowns are an NFL record by a margin so large it may never be challenged.

Quarterback · Indianapolis Colts / Broncos
Peyton Manning

2× Super Bowl Champion, 5× MVP — the most in NFL history. Manning's pre-snap intelligence revolutionized the quarterback position. His mastery of the audible made him the game's premier field general.

Wide Receiver · Minnesota Vikings / Patriots
Randy Moss

1× Super Bowl Champion. Set the single-season touchdown record with 23 in 2007. Moss' combination of size, speed, and leaping ability made him the most physically gifted wide receiver in NFL history.

Running Back · Chicago Bears
Walter Payton

"Sweetness." 1× Super Bowl Champion. Payton's 16,726 career rushing yards stood as the all-time record for 18 years. His toughness, grace, and work ethic made him the model of a complete football player.

Running Back · Cleveland Browns
Jim Brown

Considered the greatest running back of all time. Led the NFL in rushing 8 of his 9 seasons. Brown retired at his peak — and his 5.2 yards per carry career average remains the standard for running back excellence.

Tight End · New England Patriots / Buccaneers
Rob Gronkowski

4× Super Bowl Champion. The greatest tight end in NFL history — combining elite receiving ability with dominant blocking. Gronk's touchdown rate is the best of any skill position player in NFL history.

Defensive Tackle · Los Angeles Rams
Aaron Donald

1× Super Bowl Champion, 3× Defensive Player of the Year. The most disruptive interior defensive lineman in NFL history. Donald's combination of strength, speed, and technique made him virtually unblockable.

Linebacker · New York Giants
Lawrence Taylor

2× Super Bowl Champion. The most disruptive defensive player in NFL history. LT's 1986 season — 20.5 sacks — is considered the greatest defensive performance ever. He changed how offenses designed blocking schemes.

Cornerback · Multiple Teams
Deion Sanders

2× Super Bowl Champion, 8× Pro Bowl. "Prime Time" is the greatest cornerback in NFL history — and the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series. His speed and swagger made him must-watch television.

Iconic Super Bowls

Games That Defined an Era

I

Packers 35, Chiefs 10

January 15, 1967. Tickets cost $6. The first Super Bowl drew 61,946 fans — and was not sold out. Today, a Super Bowl ticket averages $10,000.

XLII

Giants 17, Patriots 14

February 3, 2008. David Tyree's helmet catch. The Giants ended the Patriots' quest for a perfect 19-0 season in one of the greatest upsets in Super Bowl history.

LI

Patriots 34, Falcons 28 OT

February 5, 2017. Tom Brady orchestrated the greatest comeback in Super Bowl history — erasing a 28-3 deficit to win in the first-ever Super Bowl overtime.

III

Jets 16, Colts 7

January 12, 1969. Joe Namath guaranteed a victory — then delivered. The AFL's triumph over the NFL establishment validated the merger and changed professional football forever.

XXIII

49ers 20, Bengals 16

January 22, 1989. Joe Montana, 92 yards, 3:10 remaining. The greatest two-minute drill in Super Bowl history ended with a TD pass to John Taylor.

LVII

Chiefs 38, Eagles 35

February 12, 2023. Patrick Mahomes overcame a slow start to lead Kansas City to victory — cementing his place among the NFL's all-time great clutch performers.
