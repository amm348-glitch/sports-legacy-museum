NBA
Basketball

Founded in 1946, the NBA became the world's premier basketball league and a global cultural force. From Naismith's peach basket to the superstar era, this is the story of the game.

By the numbers: 30 Teams— 78 Seasons— 1.3B Global Fans— $10B Annual Revenue
The Greats

NBA Legends

The players who defined eras, broke records, and made the NBA the most watched basketball league on earth.

Shooting Guard · Chicago Bulls
Michael Jordan

6× NBA Champion, 6× Finals MVP, 5× League MVP. The greatest scorer in NBA history at 30.1 PPG. Jordan redefined what winning looked like.

Small Forward · Multiple Teams
LeBron James

The NBA's all-time leading scorer. 4× NBA Champion with 3 different franchises. LeBron's longevity and versatility make him the strongest case for the greatest player ever.

Center · Los Angeles Lakers
Kareem Abdul-Jabbar

Former all-time scoring leader with 38,387 points. 6× NBA Champion, 6× MVP. His skyhook is the most unstoppable move in basketball history.

Point Guard · Los Angeles Lakers
Magic Johnson

5× NBA Champion, 3× Finals MVP. Magic's arrival in 1979 alongside Larry Bird saved a struggling league and sparked the golden era of NBA basketball.

Small Forward · Boston Celtics
Larry Bird

3× NBA Champion, 3× consecutive MVP (1984–1986). Bird's rivalry with Magic Johnson is the defining narrative of 1980s basketball.

Shooting Guard · Los Angeles Lakers
Kobe Bryant

5× NBA Champion. Kobe's Mamba Mentality — the relentless pursuit of excellence — became a cultural philosophy extending far beyond basketball.

Center · Multiple Teams
Wilt Chamberlain

Scored 100 points in a single game on March 2, 1962. Averaged 50.4 PPG for an entire season. The most dominant individual performance in NBA history.

Point Guard · Golden State Warriors
Stephen Curry

4× NBA Champion, 2× MVP. Curry revolutionized basketball by proving the three-point shot could be the primary weapon — changing the game at every level.

Small Forward · Multiple Teams
Kevin Durant

2× NBA Champion, 2× Finals MVP. One of the most gifted scorers in NBA history at 7 feet tall. Durant's ability to score from anywhere is unmatched in the modern era.

Power Forward · San Antonio Spurs
Tim Duncan

5× NBA Champion, 3× Finals MVP, 2× League MVP. "The Big Fundamental" led the Spurs dynasty for two decades — the model of consistency in professional sports.

Center · Multiple Teams
Shaquille O'Neal

4× NBA Champion, 3× Finals MVP, 1× League MVP. Shaq was the most physically dominant player of his era — combining size, power, and agility to overwhelm every center he faced.

Center · Boston Celtics
Bill Russell

11× NBA Champion — the most championships of any player in North American professional sports history. Russell's Celtics won 8 consecutive titles from 1959–1966.

Dynasty Eras

The Greatest Teams

1984–1998

Chicago Bulls

Michael Jordan's Bulls won 6 championships in 8 years. The 1995–96 team went 72-10 — a record that stood for 20 years.

1980–1988

Los Angeles Lakers — Showtime

Magic Johnson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, James Worthy. 5 championships in the 1980s with an up-tempo style that made basketball must-watch TV.

2014–2019

Golden State Warriors

Curry, Thompson, Green, and Durant. Four championships in five years. Their motion offense changed how basketball is played at every level.

1956–1969

Boston Celtics — The Original Dynasty

Bill Russell's Celtics won 11 championships in 13 years — including 8 consecutive titles. No team in professional sports history has dominated so completely.
