Why This Matters

Sport as American Identity

American sports are more than games. They are mirrors of the nation — reflecting its values, its conflicts, its triumphs, and its failures. When Jackie Robinson stepped onto Ebbets Field in 1947, it wasn't just a baseball game. It was a statement about what America could become.

When Muhammad Ali refused the draft, when Billie Jean King beat Bobby Riggs, when Colin Kaepernick took a knee — these moments transcended sport. They became part of the national conversation about who we are and what we stand for.

This museum exists to tell that story. Not just the scores and the statistics, but the human drama behind them. The sacrifice, the courage, the legacy.
